# The Path to Peace

**Understanding why we fight — and how we can stop.**

An open educational resource documenting world conflicts, their root causes, historical lessons, and evidence-based paths to lasting peace. Built for students, educators, journalists, policymakers, and every citizen who believes peace is possible.

**[View the live site](https://mikelninh.github.io/path-to-peace/)**

---

## Our Vision

**A world where every person understands why conflicts happen — and knows what actually works to end them.**

War is not inevitable. It is a choice — made by leaders, driven by systems, and sustained by narratives. Every single war in human history has eventually ended. The question is never *whether* a conflict will end, but *how* — and at what cost.

We believe that **understanding is the first step toward change.** When people see the patterns clearly — what triggers wars, who benefits, who suffers, and what has actually worked to bring peace — they gain the power to demand better from their leaders and from themselves.

This project exists because the truth about conflict and peace should be:
- **Accessible to everyone** — not locked in academic journals or think-tank reports
- **Visual and intuitive** — complex doesn't have to mean confusing
- **Honest and balanced** — showing all sides, naming root causes, rejecting propaganda
- **Actionable** — not just information, but a guide to what each person can do
- **Free forever** — peace education should never have a paywall

## Our Goal

**To become the world's most trusted, accessible, and useful open resource for understanding conflict and building peace — used in schools, referenced by governments, and shared by citizens worldwide.**

Specifically:
1. **Document every active conflict** with honest, detailed analysis of who, why, what, and how
2. **Connect history to the present** — showing what we learned from past wars and how those lessons apply today
3. **Make the path to peace visible** — evidence-based strategies that have actually worked, not wishful thinking
4. **Empower action** — giving every person concrete steps they can take, from the classroom to the ballot box
5. **Build institutional trust** — through transparent sourcing, academic rigor, and open governance

## What's Inside (v1.0)

- **15 current conflicts** — each with detailed analysis: who is fighting, why, what resources are at stake, what it's really about, and paths to resolution
- **8 root cause categories** with deep dives and real-world examples
- **10 historical conflicts** — how they started, how they ended, and what we learned
- **6 proven mechanisms** for how wars actually end (with historical success rates)
- **6 observable patterns** across all conflicts throughout history
- **6 evidence-based strategies** for building lasting peace
- **6 "history applied to today" analyses** — mapping past solutions to current conflicts
- **6 concrete action guides** — for citizens, leaders, educators, consumers, and communities

## How It's Built

Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — **zero dependencies, zero frameworks, zero tracking**. The entire site is four files:

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `index.html` | Page structure and sections |
| `styles.css` | Dark theme design system with responsive layout |
| `data.js` | All conflict data, historical lessons, and action guides |
| `app.js` | Rendering, interactivity, filters, modal, and animations |

This is intentional. The site should be:
- **Deployable anywhere** — any static host, any country, any network
- **Fast** — no build step, no CDN dependencies, loads instantly
- **Forkable** — anyone can take this and adapt it
- **Censorship-resistant** — simple enough to mirror and redistribute

## Sources & Methodology

All content is sourced from established, peer-reviewed conflict research:

- **[Uppsala Conflict Data Program (UCDP)](https://ucdp.uu.se/)** — The academic gold standard for conflict data (40+ years)
- **[Armed Conflict Location & Event Data (ACLED)](https://acleddata.com/)** — Real-time, granular event-level conflict data
- **[Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI)](https://www.sipri.org/)** — Arms transfers, military expenditure, arms control
- **[International Crisis Group (ICG)](https://www.crisisgroup.org/)** — Field-based qualitative conflict analysis
- **[United Nations OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/)** — Humanitarian data and displacement figures
- **[UNHCR](https://www.unhcr.org/)** — Refugee and displacement statistics

We follow these principles:
1. **No original claims** — we synthesize and present established research, not our own analysis
2. **Transparent sourcing** — every fact should be traceable to an authoritative source
3. **Balanced perspective** — we present all sides of each conflict honestly
4. **Regular updates** — conflict situations change; our content must keep pace
5. **Open correction** — if something is wrong, we fix it publicly and promptly
